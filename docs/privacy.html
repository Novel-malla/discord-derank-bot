Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Derank Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and manages information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the Bot may process the following information provided through Discord:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username
  • Discord display name
  • Discord avatar information
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Discord channel and thread IDs
  • LFG participation information
  • XP and level information
  • Ticket information and ticket status

3. Messages

The Bot may receive Discord message events when required for features such as the XP system.

The Bot does not intentionally store the full content of ordinary messages for the XP system. Message activity may be used to determine whether XP should be awarded.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by the Bot is used only to provide and improve the Bot's functionality.

Examples include:

  • Maintaining XP and level information
  • Displaying leaderboards
  • Managing LFG posts and participants
  • Creating and managing support tickets
  • Maintaining server-related Bot functionality

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information processed by the Bot is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the Bot or where required by law.

The Bot operates using Discord's APIs and infrastructure. Information made available through Discord is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage

Some Bot data is stored in a database hosted on infrastructure used to operate the Bot.

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality or as required for legitimate operational, legal, or security purposes.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information stored by the Bot.

To request deletion, contact the Bot developer through the Bot's support server or the contact method provided by the Bot developer.

When a valid deletion request is received, applicable personal data will be deleted unless retention is required by law or is reasonably necessary for security or legitimate operational purposes.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is operated through Discord and is subject to Discord's age requirements and policies. We do not knowingly collect additional personal information from children outside of information made available through Discord.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Bot developer through the support server or the contact method provided by the Bot developer.

12. Discord

Derank Bot is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.
